## Entry

A single deadpan TV weather forecast, one line long, for Brimwick — a city that does not exist. The forecaster reports a steady fall of teaspoons, measured in millimetres per hour like ordinary rain, gives the temperature and wind speed in the usual flat broadcast tone, and ends with a perfectly routine safety note: wear a helmet and park the car indoors. The joke is the contrast — nothing in the delivery admits that teaspoons falling from the sky is strange.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the forecast to `/output/entry.txt`. It needs no dependencies and no network access, and it produces the same line on every run.
